# Video selection checklist (~20s soccer clip)

## Requirements
- Duration between 18 and 25 seconds
- Camera view shows enough of the pitch to infer field geometry
- Player movement includes both teams in multiple field zones
- Ball is visible for a meaningful part of the clip
- License/usage permission is known and recorded
